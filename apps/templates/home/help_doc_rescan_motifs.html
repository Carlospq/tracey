What it does — Re-runs the HMMER (pyhmmer) domain scan for the selected sequences against the chosen HMM profile(s), and updates the Motifs table in TRACEY accordingly. Unlike "Update TRACEY Sequences", this never contacts NCBI — it only re-classifies sequences that are already in TRACEY using the current HMM models. Use it after adding or editing HMM profiles, changing the e-value cutoff, or to fix motif calls without a full NCBI sync.


How a hit is applied:

  • A hit that doesn't overlap any existing motif on the sequence is added as new.
  • A hit that overlaps an existing motif of the same domain replaces it only if its e-value is better; the replaced motif is kept for reference in Verifymotifs rather than deleted.
  • A hit that overlaps a motif of a different domain is skipped, to avoid conflicting calls on the same region.
  • Hits shorter than the minimum expected length for that HMM are discarded.
  • Per-sequence limits are enforced automatically: at most 1 Habc and 1 Longin domain (and the two are mutually exclusive on the same sequence), at most 1 non-SNAP SNARE domain (Qa/Qb/Qc/R), and at most 2 SNAP domains.

Species filter (optional) — Restrict scanning to one taxon by shortname (e.g. HoSa) or scientific name; all of its descendant taxa are included automatically (e.g. selecting a genus scans every species under it). Leave both boxes empty to consider matching sequences from every species in TRACEY.


HMM scope (required — pick one):

  • Protein family — scans against every HMM belonging to that family (e.g. all SNARE profiles).
  • Domain — becomes available once a family is chosen; narrows the scan to one domain group under that family (and everything nested below it).
  • Specific HMM key — free-text box with autocomplete; type an exact key from the HMM menu (e.g. Longin.I) to scan against just that node and its children. When filled in, this overrides the Protein family / Domain selection.

E-value — Significance cutoff passed to hmmscan (default shown: 1e-10). Only hits below this threshold are kept; lower values are stricter and yield fewer, more confident hits.


Motif filter (optional) — A performance shortcut: instead of scanning every sequence that falls under the chosen HMM scope, only scan sequences that already have a motif of the selected domain in TRACEY. Useful to avoid wasting time re-scanning sequences that were never candidates for that family in the first place.


Only active sequences — Skip any sequence whose status is not "live" (e.g. dead, replaced, ignored).


Dry run — Opens a new tab listing the planned action for every matching sequence (NEW / UPDATE / SKIP-CONFLICT / SKIP-LIMIT / SKIP-LEN / ...) without writing anything to the database. Recommended before running for real, especially on a broad family-wide scope.
