To search the database select one taxonomic group from the box on the left, and any of the sub-taxas from the boxes below.

A list of species included in the selected taxas will be displayed on the right.

You can also choose multiple species by holding "Shift" + left click.

To remove the mark of a species press "Ctrl" while clicking on the marked species.

If no species is selected then the search will be done for all of them.
