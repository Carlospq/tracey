Shortname represents species and genus.

For example:
HoSa:Homo sapiens
MuMu:Mus musculus
RaNo:Rattus norvegicus
DaRe:Danio rerio
DrMe:Drosophila melanogaster
CaEl:Caenorhabditis elegans
MoBr:Monosiga brevicollis
SaCe:Saccharomyces cerevisiae
ArTh:Arabidopsis thaliana
ChRe:Chlamydomonas reinhardtii

It can also contain information about the sequence type. This second part of the shortname is separated by underscore "_".
For example:
HoSa_Syx1
HoSa_Syx8
